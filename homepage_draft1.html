Who We Are

Research That Changes
Lives & Communities

CEHDR is an interdisciplinary research center based in the UC Irvine Joe C. Wen School of Population and Public Health, founded through the UCI Black Thriving Initiative to expand scholarship focused on the experiences of Black American communities and environmental justice.

Directed by Dr. Karen D. Lincoln, our center brings together 24+ affiliated faculty spanning Public Health, Medicine, Civil & Environmental Engineering, Urban Planning, Nursing, Anthropology, and Pharmacy.

"To move forward, we must listen and address the issues African American communities have identified as problems and priorities." — Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

Four Initiatives. One Powerful Mission.

Every gift supports groundbreaking work across all four CEHDR pillars — or you can direct your support to a specific initiative that resonates with you.

🌱
THRIVE
Transforming Health Research through Inclusion, Voices & Equity

Ensuring every voice matters in shaping environmental health research, especially from communities historically underrepresented in science.

🎓
RISE
Research Internships for Social & Environmental Health Equity

Cultivating the next generation of leaders through hands-on, community-engaged research experiences and student mentorship.

🤝
CIRCLE
Community Initiatives for Research, Collaboration, Learning & Engagement

Strengthening partnerships between CEHDR and communities to co-create research and drive solutions to environmental health challenges.

💡
IMPACT
Innovative Minds Promoting Action, Collaboration & Transformation

A Distinguished Speaker Series spotlighting transformative thinkers whose leadership drives meaningful change in environmental health.
