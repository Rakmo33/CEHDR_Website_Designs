Our Initiatives

Four Initiatives. One Powerful Mission.

Every gift supports groundbreaking work across all four CEHDR pillars — or you can direct your support to a specific initiative that resonates with you.

🌱
THRIVE

Transforming Health Research through Inclusion, Voices, and Equity

CEHDR THRIVE ensures that every voice matters in shaping environmental health research, especially those from communities historically underrepresented in science. Donor support will fund community engagement, inclusive educational programming, and equity-centered training programs.

🎓
RISE

Research Internships for Social & Environmental Health Equity

CEHDR RISE cultivates the next generation of leaders in environmental health equity by providing students with hands-on, community-engaged research experiences. Donations support student stipends, mentorship, and collaborative projects with local communities.

🤝
CIRCLE

Community Initiatives for Research, Collaboration, Learning, and Engagement

CEHDR CIRCLE strengthens partnerships between the Center and communities to co-create research, share knowledge, and drive solutions to environmental health challenges. Funding supports collaborative projects, workshops, and community-driven interventions.

💡
IMPACT

Innovative Minds Promoting Action, Collaboration, and Transformation

The CEHDR IMPACT Distinguished Speaker Series spotlights transformative thinkers whose research and leadership drive meaningful change in environmental health and science. Your gift creates a vibrant academic environment where knowledge sparks action.

How to Give

Your Donation, Your Choice

Every contribution — no matter the size — makes a meaningful difference. Follow the steps below to direct your gift online or by check.

💻 Giving Online

  1. Click the "Give Today" button to access the UCI donation portal.
  2. Select the Joe C. Wen School of Population & Public Health – Public Health Centers Fund as your giving area.
  3. To direct your gift to a specific initiative, enter one of the following names in the "Comment" field:
    🌱 CEHDR THRIVE 🎓 CEHDR RISE 🤝 CEHDR CIRCLE 💡 CEHDR IMPACT
  4. Optionally, honor someone special using the "Honor / Memory / Support of Someone" field.
  5. Complete your gift — and thank you for your generosity!
