Who We Are

An Interdisciplinary Center for Justice & Health Equity

CEHDR was founded in 2021 as a result of the UCI Black Thriving Initiative Faculty Cluster Hiring Program to expand scholarship focused on the experiences of Black American communities and environmental justice.

Our 24+ affiliated faculty span 8 disciplines — Public Health, Medicine, Civil & Environmental Engineering, Urban Planning, Nursing, Anthropology, and Pharmacy — all committed to creating a healthier, more equitable world.

"Through CEHDR CIRCLE, we are building sustainable, community-centered approaches to resilience that not only improve disaster response capacity, but train the next generation of public health leaders." — Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

Our Four Signature Initiatives

Every gift powers groundbreaking work — or direct your support to the initiative that resonates most.

🌱
THRIVE

Transforming Health Research through Inclusion, Voices & Equity

CEHDR THRIVE ensures that every voice matters in shaping environmental health research, especially from communities historically underrepresented in science. Donor support funds community engagement, inclusive educational programming, and equity-centered training.

🎁 Support THRIVE
🎓
RISE

Research Internships for Social & Environmental Health Equity

CEHDR RISE cultivates the next generation of leaders in environmental health equity by providing students with hands-on, community-engaged research experiences. Donations support student stipends, mentorship, and collaborative projects with local communities.

🎁 Support RISE
🤝
CIRCLE

Community Initiatives for Research, Collaboration, Learning & Engagement

CEHDR CIRCLE strengthens partnerships between the Center and communities to co-create research, share knowledge, and drive solutions to environmental health challenges through collaborative workshops and interventions.

🎁 Support CIRCLE
💡
IMPACT

Innovative Minds Promoting Action, Collaboration & Transformation

The CEHDR IMPACT Distinguished Speaker Series spotlights transformative thinkers whose research and leadership drive meaningful change. Your gift creates a vibrant academic environment where knowledge sparks action.

🎁 Support IMPACT
