# Preparedness booklet and “This Is Me” insert

Review draft for Dr. Gaines, August 28, 2026. Not yet clinically approved or physically test-printed.

## Files and printing

- Emergency-Preparedness-Booklet.pdf: 20 pages in reading order, including covers; each page is 5.5 x 8.5 inches. Main checklist text is 16 pt Arial; safety notes are 15 pt. Headings are 25 pt. Credits and footers are smaller.
- Booklet-Letter-Duplex-Print.pdf: already arranged for five US Letter sheets. Print landscape, actual size / 100%, double-sided, flip on the short edge. Do not select a second booklet or multiple-pages-per-sheet setting. Nest the sheets, fold at the center and saddle-staple. Check page order with one test copy first.
- This-Is-Me-Card.pdf: four panels in reading order, each 5 x 8 inches. Field labels are 15 pt, instructions mostly 14 pt. Ruled writing spaces have approximately 0.3-inch spacing.
- This-Is-Me-Letter-Duplex-Print.pdf: one Letter sheet, two sides. Print landscape, actual size / 100%, double-sided, flip on the short edge. Trim at the corner marks to 10 x 8 inches; fold at the center tick marks to 5 x 8 inches. The “This Is Me” panel is the front. Do not cut down the center.

Use opaque, uncoated paper for readability and handwriting. A heavier uncoated insert stock may be used if supported by the printer; score before folding. Do not laminate before the card is filled in. A protective sleeve is an alternative. These files have no bleed requirement. Printer-specific feed direction and duplex alignment still need a physical test.

## Editorial changes requiring approval

This is a plain-language adaptation, not a verbatim transcription. All major source sections are retained; duplicate incontinence supplies and repeated items were consolidated. Some long lists of examples were shortened. The source's six dense pages became twenty small-format pages so the body text could remain readable. The separate card was newly designed; the source describes a card but does not supply a complete form.

- Medications: retained the 7–14 day planning target, but qualified it with prescriber/pharmacist advice and availability. Removed the blanket list of over-the-counter medicines; substituted a safety check with the care team. Added individualized storage and fluid guidance.
- Generator wording: separated fuel-burning generators from battery power stations. Specified outdoor use at least 20 feet from doors, windows and vents for fuel generators.
- Flooding: removed plastic sheeting and tape as an implied flood barrier. Added a warning against entering floodwater.
- Heat: added an air-conditioned destination and cautioned against relying only on a fan.
- Alerts: routed readers through the official Orange County emergency page because county pages use inconsistent AlertOC domains. Replaced the unverified “AFN registry confirmation” requirement with advice to ask emergency management about available disability and transport support.
- Wandering: removed the source's conflation of GPS registration and MedicAlert/Safe Return. Avoided reproducing potentially outdated program branding and enrollment numbers. Added that devices do not replace supervision.
- Support numbers: verified Alzheimer’s Orange County, Alzheimer’s Association, Caregiver Resource Center OC and SCE. Only the Alzheimer’s Association is labeled 24/7. Removed an unverified promise of free emergency-preparedness consultations. Omitted the county disaster public hotline from the compact directory; 911/211 and official county information remain.
- Identification: source home-address and full-medication details are not copied onto the new personal-support card. The card instead carries emergency contacts and the location of a separate current medicine list. Private identity/financial documents are kept separately. Confirm whether Dr. Gaines wants additional identification fields.
- Caregiver medicine quantity was consolidated into the general medication-planning guidance rather than retaining a separate 3–7 day target.
- Source institution/address/email/web attribution was retained as provided. Please confirm these details and institutional approval before distribution. No official logo has been fabricated.
