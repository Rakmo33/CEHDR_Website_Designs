ABOUT THE EVENT

The CEHDR Community Forum on Healthy Longevity, Caregiving, and Emergency Preparedness will bring together approximately 200 older adults, family caregivers, healthcare professionals, community organizations, faith leaders, and local stakeholders from across Orange County. The event will promote healthy longevity, strengthen caregiver support, increase access to community resources, and improve emergency preparedness among vulnerable populations. All attendees will receive emergency preparedness kits and educational materials.

ABOUT AAAE

Since 2012, Advocates for African American Elders (AAAE) has been a trusted voice for African American adults 45+ in Los Angeles County and, since 2023, in Orange County. AAAE raises awareness, increases knowledge and access to healthcare resources, and improves health outcomes through education, outreach, research, and advocacy.

Through forums, talk shows, fact sheets, and community partnerships, AAAE has educated thousands, enrolled over 1,500 African Americans into research studies, and advanced brain health awareness and Alzheimer’s disease education for more than a decade.

ABOUT CEHDR

The Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research (CEHDR) at the UCI Joe C. Wen School of Population and Public Health advances health equity through community-engaged research, education, and action. In just three years, CEHDR has built a strong foundation for impact:

500+Community members engaged through public forums and distinguished lectures
25+Community and cross-sector partnerships
10+Student fellows trained in research and public health
$1.8M+External funding leveraged since 2023

CEHDR’s signature initiatives—RISE, THRIVE, CIRCLE, and IMPACT—center community voices at the heart of research and action, driving real solutions for healthier communities.

ABOUT THE UCI FOUNDATION

The UCI Foundation raises and manages private funds to support the University of California, Irvine’s mission of excellence in teaching, research, and public service. Gifts to the UCI Foundation are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.
