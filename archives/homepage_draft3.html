Our Mission

Why CEHDR Exists

We bridge rigorous science and community action to understand and reduce environmental health disparities facing low-income and minority populations.

🔭

Cutting-Edge Research

We conduct rigorous, interdisciplinary research examining the links between environmental exposures and health outcomes in underserved communities across Southern California.

🤝

Community Partnership

We work alongside community members and advocacy groups to ensure research questions reflect real needs — and that findings translate into meaningful policy change.

🎓

Training the Next Generation

CEHDR mentors graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career scientists passionate about environmental justice and public health equity.

Research Focus

Our Key Research Areas

From air pollution to climate change, our interdisciplinary teams tackle the environmental drivers of health inequity.

🌬️

Air Quality & Respiratory Health

Investigating particulate matter, ozone, and traffic-related air pollution and their disproportionate impact on communities of color and children with asthma.

20+ Active Studies
☠️

Toxic Exposures & Cumulative Risk

Evaluating combined environmental and socioeconomic burdens through CalEnviroScreen-informed methodologies and biomonitoring programs.

Statewide Impact
🌡️

Climate Change & Health Equity

Examining how extreme heat, wildfires, and flooding exacerbate health disparities in already-vulnerable communities, with focus on adaptive solutions.

NIH Funded
👶

Children's Environmental Health

Longitudinal cohort studies on prenatal and early-life exposures and their influence on neurodevelopmental, respiratory, and metabolic outcomes.

Cohort Studies
