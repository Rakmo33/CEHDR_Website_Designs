Who We Are

An Interdisciplinary Center for Justice & Health Equity

The Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research (CEHDR) is based in the UC Irvine Joe C. Wen School of Population and Public Health, founded in 2021 through the UCI Black Thriving Initiative to advance environmental justice scholarship.

Our 24+ affiliated faculty span 8 disciplines — Public Health, Medicine, Civil & Environmental Engineering, Urban Planning, Nursing, Anthropology, and Pharmacy — all united by a commitment to health equity.

"To move forward, we must listen and address the issues African American residents, communities, and organizations have identified as problems and priorities." — Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

Support CEHDR

Four Initiatives. One Powerful Mission.

Every gift supports groundbreaking work across all four CEHDR pillars — or direct your support to a specific initiative that resonates most with you.

THRIVE

Transforming Health Research through Inclusion, Voices & Equity

CEHDR THRIVE ensures that every voice matters in shaping environmental health research. Donor support funds community engagement, inclusive educational programming, and equity-centered training programs.

Support THRIVE
RISE

Research Internships for Social & Environmental Health Equity

CEHDR RISE cultivates the next generation of leaders through hands-on, community-engaged research experiences. Donations support student stipends, mentorship, and collaborative projects.

Support RISE
CIRCLE

Community Initiatives for Research, Collaboration, Learning & Engagement

CEHDR CIRCLE strengthens partnerships between the Center and communities to co-create research and drive solutions to environmental health challenges through collaborative workshops.

Support CIRCLE
IMPACT

Innovative Minds Promoting Action, Collaboration & Transformation

The CEHDR IMPACT Distinguished Speaker Series spotlights transformative thinkers whose research and leadership drive meaningful change in environmental health and science.
