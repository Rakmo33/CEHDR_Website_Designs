A MESSAGE FROM THE DIRECTOR

Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

Director, Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research (CEHDR)

“Health equity is not achieved through research alone. It is achieved when research is informed by community voices, translated into meaningful action, and used to create opportunities for all people to live healthier lives. At CEHDR, we are committed to building that bridge between science and community.”

  • As we celebrate Juneteenth and reflect on the ongoing pursuit of equity, we are reminded that meaningful change begins by listening, learning, and working alongside communities.
  • This issue highlights an exciting season of growth for CEHDR, including our newest team members, newly launched website, expanded social media presence, community partnerships, and research initiatives.
  • We are proud to feature highlights from our Joy & Pain study, which examines how social determinants and environmental experiences shape health and well-being among Black Americans.
  • Through our Citizen Panels, the CEHDR RISE Fellowship Program, and other community-engaged initiatives, we continue creating opportunities for community members, researchers, and students to learn from one another and co-create solutions.
  • We are grateful for the support of our community partners, students, faculty affiliates, supporters, and the Pacific Life Foundation.
  • Thank you for being part of the CEHDR community. We invite you to explore this issue, connect with us online, and join us as we continue advancing research, education, and action for healthier communities.
