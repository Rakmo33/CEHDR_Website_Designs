How Engagement Creates Change

From lived experience to shared action

Community engagement is a continuous exchange. Each stage informs the next, and findings return to the people who helped create the knowledge.

01

Listen

Residents identify experiences, assets, barriers, and priorities.

02

Learn Together

Accessible formats create space for questions and shared understanding.

03

Set Priorities

Community members interpret evidence and determine what matters most.

04

Shape Research

Priorities guide study design, recruitment, programs, and partnerships.

05

Return Results

Findings are shared in useful, culturally responsive, and actionable ways.
