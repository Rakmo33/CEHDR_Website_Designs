Meet your host and speakers

A live community talk show bringing together perspectives on healthy aging, caregiving, brain health, and disaster preparedness.

Your host

Karen D. Lincoln

PhD, MSW, MA, FGSA, FAASWW

Professor & Director, CEHDR, UC Irvine

Public health leader and sociologist advancing health equity through research, policy, and advocacy, with a focus on Black older adults, women, and communities affected by inequitable care.

Guest speaker

Tia Delaney-Stewart

MSN-Ed, RN, PHN

National Director of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Engagement, Alzheimer's Association

Nurse leader with over 30 years of experience in home health and hospice, supporting older adults and healthy aging through engagement and advocacy.

Guest speaker

Kacie Deters

PhD

Assistant Professor of Integrative Biology and Physiology, UCLA

Neuroscientist specializing in cognitive decline, Alzheimer's disease, and racial disparities in brain health. Her lab leads community-engaged research and education for older adults in Black communities.

Guest speaker

Andrew Grundig

LAFD CERT Call-Out Team Member; Former Battalion 6 Coordinator, Los Angeles Fire Department

Public safety advocate and disaster response leader who trains community members in personal preparedness, emergency workflows, and disaster resilience.
