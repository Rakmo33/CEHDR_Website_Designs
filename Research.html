Advancing Environmental Justice

We conduct research to advance environmental justice by addressing environmental health inequities resulting from environmental racism and harmful environmental exposures. Our work focuses on educating the public, empowering communities, and developing sustainable interventions to reduce disparities in health impacts disproportionately affecting minoritized communities. Through community-driven research and advocacy, we aim to challenge systemic injustices, inform policy change, and create healthier, more equitable environments for all.

Community

Research is strongest when it is shaped by the people it seeks to serve. We partner with residents, community organizations, health systems, and public leaders to ensure that community voices inform every stage of the research process. Through community-engaged research and collaborative partnerships, we work to develop solutions that are relevant, responsive, and rooted in lived experience.

Areas of Focus
  • Community-engaged research
  • Citizen panels
  • Community advisory boards
  • Research translation & dissemination
  • Public engagement & partnership development

Place

Health is shaped by the environments where people live, work, learn, play, and age. We study how neighborhood conditions, environmental exposures, climate-related risks, and access to community resources influence health and contribute to inequities across populations. Our goal is to understand how place shapes opportunity and well-being throughout the lifespan.

Areas of Focus
  • Environmental exposures
  • Neighborhood conditions
  • Air quality and pollution
  • Climate and health
  • Housing & the built environment

Equity

Achieving health equity requires understanding and addressing the systems and structures that shape health. Our research examines how social, economic, political, and environmental conditions influence health outcomes and contribute to disparities. By identifying the root causes of inequities, we generate evidence that supports more equitable policies, programs, and practices.

Areas of Focus
  • Social determinants of health
  • Structural determinants of health
  • Health disparities
  • Racial and ethnic inequities
  • Population health

Justice

All communities deserve the opportunity to live in safe, healthy, and sustainable environments. We work alongside communities disproportionately affected by environmental hazards and social inequities to advance environmental justice through research, partnership, advocacy, and action. Our work seeks to transform evidence into meaningful change that improves health and quality of life.

Areas of Focus
  • Environmental justice
  • Community advocacy
  • Policy and systems change
  • Environmental racism
  • Community-driven solutions

Well-Being

Health is more than the absence of disease. We investigate the factors that influence physical, mental, cognitive, and emotional well-being across the lifespan. From brain health and healthy aging to sleep health, stress, and chronic disease, our research seeks to understand the conditions that help individuals and communities thrive.

Areas of Focus
  • Brain health
  • Healthy aging and longevity
  • Sleep health
  • Stress and psychosocial factors
  • Chronic disease prevention & management
