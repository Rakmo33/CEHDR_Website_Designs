Our Founding Story

Built for Communities. Grounded in Trust.

Est. 2021 · Black Thriving Initiative

The Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research (CEHDR) was established in 2021 through UC Irvine's Black Thriving Initiative Faculty Cluster Hiring Program with a bold and explicit mission: to expand scholarship on the health experiences of Black American communities as they relate to environmental justice.

From its very founding, CEHDR was designed not as a traditional academic research unit, but as a community-facing center — one where research questions emerge from the communities most affected by environmental health disparities, and where findings are translated directly back through education, advocacy, and action.

Based in the Joe C. Wen School of Population and Public Health within the Susan and Henry Samueli College of Health Sciences at UC Irvine, CEHDR has grown from a newly established center into a recognized hub for community-engaged environmental health disparities research.

What Drives Us

Vision, Mission & Values

Three commitments that anchor every partnership, every study, and every program at CEHDR.

Vision

A Just and Equitable Society

An environmentally and socially just society where Black and historically marginalized communities thrive with health equity — where every community enjoys the same protections from environmental health hazards and equal access to decision-making.

Mission

Research That Creates Change

To build research, educational, and community programs that support health equity and social justice — translating rigorous science directly into meaningful action within the communities it is designed to serve.

Values

Equity. Belonging. Community.

CEHDR's actions are centered on equity, belonging, and community collaboration. We champion justice for communities made vulnerable by environmental injustice — grounding every partnership, every study, and every program in these core commitments.
