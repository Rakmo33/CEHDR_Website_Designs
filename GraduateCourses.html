PUBHLTH 250
Health Status and Care Disparities
Political Determinants Population Health PDOH Framework

Why do some communities get sicker than others? Discover that clinical care and biology account for only 20% of health outcomes — the remaining 80% is driven by environment, social factors, and behavior that we can actually change. Using the Political Determinants of Health (PDOH) framework, you will move from understanding to action.

Life Course & Intergenerational Focus

This course integrates life course approaches to health disparities, emphasizing both developmental and structural perspectives on how exposures across the lifespan, and across generations, shape population health outcomes. Students examine how socially patterned exposures accumulate and interact, and how interventions can be designed with timing, context, and intergenerational impacts in mind.

What You Will Do

Analyze the 80% of determinants driving health inequities
Apply the PDOH framework to real community challenges
Design actionable solutions for your target population
Examine how exposures across the lifespan and across generations shape population health outcomes

PUBHLTH 279
Environmental Health Disparities
Environmental Justice Structural Factors Health Equity

How does the environment literally get "under the skin"? Examine how physical and social environments shape outcomes — especially for minoritized communities. Through the Exposure–Disease–Stress Model and the Environmental Affordances Model, you will uncover structural determinants of opportunity and develop real strategies to close the gaps.

What You Will Do

Examine how environmental factors drive health disparities
Critically evaluate structural determinants shaping communities
Develop evidence-based interventions for environmental equity
