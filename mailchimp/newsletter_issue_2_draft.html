PhD · MSW · MA · FGSA · FAASWSW

Professor of Environmental & Occupational Health
Director of Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research

Expanding Our Reach, Deepening Our Impact

As we enter the fall season, CEHDR is building exciting momentum. Our recent community disaster preparedness event was a tremendous success, bringing together community members, organizations, and students while generating new partnerships, sponsors, and volunteers. This growing network reflects the power of collaboration and our shared commitment to advancing environmental health equity.

CEHDR’s expanding reach is also reflected in our growing body of scholarship, increased media visibility, and opportunities to bring our research and expertise to broader audiences. These accomplishments underscore the relevance and urgency of our work and the importance of connecting research, community knowledge, and action.

I am deeply grateful to our community partners, faculty collaborators, students, volunteers, sponsors, and supporters. Your expertise, commitment, and generosity are essential to CEHDR’s growth and impact. The future is bright, and we’re just getting started. As we enter the next phase of CEHDR’s work, I look forward to expanding collaborations, creating new opportunities for engagement and leadership, and welcoming individuals and organizations who share our vision.

I invite you to explore this issue, stay connected, and join us in advancing a more environmentally and socially just future where all communities thrive with health equity.

Thank you for being part of the CEHDR community!

— Karen D. Lincoln

Professor of Environmental & Occupational Health
Director of Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research
