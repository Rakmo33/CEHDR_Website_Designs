Research

Questions that connect systems, places, and people.

Dr. Lincoln’s research examines the psychosocial, behavioral, and biological mechanisms linking social determinants to health and well-being.

Research agenda

Five lenses.
One pursuit.

Each area informs the others, creating a fuller account of health across environments and the life course.

Environmental racism and environmental health equity

We investigate how environmental exposures, neighborhood conditions, structural inequities, and discrimination become embodied as unequal health risks and outcomes.

Brain health, Alzheimer’s disease, and dementia

Our work advances understanding of cognitive aging while strengthening research participation and brain health education in Black communities.

Sleep health and healthy longevity

We study social and psychosocial influences on sleep and the ways sleep connects with cognition, caregiving, stress, and long-term well-being.

Stress, mental health, and social relationships

Research explores discrimination, social support, social interactions, depression, and the protective and harmful dimensions of relationships.

Community-engaged research

Community knowledge is part of the research process, from identifying priorities to interpreting findings and translating evidence into accessible education and action.
