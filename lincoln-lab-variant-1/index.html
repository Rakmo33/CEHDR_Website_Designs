Lincoln Lab for Health Equity · UC Irvine

Research that moves from evidence to equity.

The Lincoln Lab investigates the social and environmental conditions that shape health across the life course, with scholarship rooted in Black American communities, older adults, and populations made vulnerable by inequitable systems.
