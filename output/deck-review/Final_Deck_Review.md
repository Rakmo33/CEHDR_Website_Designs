## Font alternatives for every slide

Use a consistent scheme across the deck, not a different family on every slide. Font pairs below mean heading / body. Option 1 is the polished choice, option 2 is the modern Office choice, and option 3 is the conservative compatibility choice.

- Avenir Next LT Pro Bold / Regular: polished and close to the clean Canva-style sans serif. It is an Office cloud font, but must be available on the event computer.
- Aptos Display Bold / Aptos: modern Microsoft Office look. Availability depends on Office version and font installation.
- Arial Bold / Arial: safest practical fallback among these choices, but still test layout after replacement.
- Condensed display alternatives are for short opening headlines only, not paragraphs.
- Do not replace the official UCI/partner logo lettering.
- Slides 20–25, 30, 32, and 58 contain lettering inside images. Those fonts can only be changed by rebuilding or replacing the image. The options listed apply to recreated text or new captions. Slides 5 and 29 contain video, so font alternatives apply to optional overlay titles/captions, not to text already burned into the video.
- [Microsoft’s Office cloud-font list](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/fonts/cloud-fonts-in-office) includes the cloud-font families discussed here. That listing does not guarantee offline availability on your event laptop.
