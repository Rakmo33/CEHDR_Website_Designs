What we study

Health inequity is connected. Our research is too.

The redesigned structure brings Dr. Lincoln’s interconnected scholarship into one visual system rather than treating each topic as a separate silo.

01 · Core lens

Environmental Racism & Environmental Health Equity

Examining how place, exposure, discrimination, and structural conditions shape unequal health risks and outcomes.

02

Brain Health, Alzheimer’s & Dementia

Research and community education focused on brain health, cognitive aging, Alzheimer’s disease, and participation in research among Black communities.

03

Sleep Health

Understanding the social and psychosocial pathways that influence sleep, brain health, and healthy longevity.

04

Stress, Mental Health & Social Relationships

Studying discrimination, social support, social interactions, depression, and well-being across the life course.

05

Community-Engaged Research

Building culturally rooted partnerships that connect research, education, advocacy, and action.

How we work

2

Lab Director

Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

Karen D. Lincoln, PhD, MSW, MA, FGSA, FAASWSW, is Professor in the Department of Environmental and Occupational Health at UC Irvine’s Joe C. Wen School of Population & Public Health and Director of the Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research. She is also Founder and Director of Advocates for African American Elders.

Her scholarship spans environmental and social determinants of health, racial health disparities, aging, brain health, sleep, mental health, social relationships, and community-engaged research.

Advocates for African American Elders

Culturally rooted. Community powered.

Founded by Dr. Karen Lincoln in 2012, AAAE provides health education for older Black adults across Los Angeles, Riverside, and Orange Counties. Together with community partners, the program promotes brain health and works to eliminate racial health disparities.
