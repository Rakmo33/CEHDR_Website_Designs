Who We Are

An Interdisciplinary Center for Justice & Health Equity

Est. 2021Black Thriving Initiative

The Center for Environmental Health Disparities Research (CEHDR) is based in the UC Irvine Joe C. Wen School of Population and Public Health, founded in 2021 through the UCI Black Thriving Initiative to advance environmental justice scholarship.

Our 24+ affiliated faculty span 8 disciplines — Public Health, Medicine, Civil & Environmental Engineering, Urban Planning, Nursing, Anthropology, and Pharmacy — all united by a commitment to health equity.

"To move forward, we must listen and address the issues African American residents, communities, and organizations have identified as problems and priorities." — Dr. Karen D. Lincoln

 Our Research Framework

Five Themes. One Mission.

Our research advances environmental justice by addressing health inequities rooted in environmental racism and harmful exposures. Explore the five interconnected themes that guide our community-driven work toward healthier, more equitable environments for all.

Community

Partnering with residents, organizations, and leaders so community voices shape every stage of the research process.

Learn More

Place

Studying how neighborhoods, environmental exposures, and climate risks shape health across the lifespan.

Learn More

Equity

Examining the social, economic, and structural conditions that drive health disparities — and the evidence to address them.

Learn More

Justice

Working alongside affected communities to advance environmental justice through research, advocacy, and action.

Learn More

Well-Being

Investigating brain health, healthy aging, sleep, and stress — the conditions that help communities thrive.

Support CEHDR

Four Initiatives. One Powerful Mission.

Every gift supports groundbreaking work across all four CEHDR pillars — or direct your support to a specific initiative.

Support an Initiative
THRIVE

Transforming Health Research through Inclusion, Voices & Equity

CEHDR THRIVE ensures every voice matters in shaping environmental health research. Donor support funds community engagement, inclusive programming, and equity-centered training.

RISE

Research Internships for Social & Environmental Health Equity

CEHDR RISE cultivates the next generation of leaders through hands-on, community-engaged research experiences. Donations support student stipends and mentorship.

CIRCLE

Community Initiatives for Research, Collaboration, Learning & Engagement

CEHDR CIRCLE strengthens partnerships between the Center and communities to co-create research and drive solutions through collaborative workshops.

IMPACT

Innovative Minds Promoting Action, Collaboration & Transformation

The CEHDR IMPACT Distinguished Speaker Series spotlights transformative thinkers whose research and leadership drive meaningful change in environmental health.
